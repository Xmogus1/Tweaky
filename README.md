# Tweaky

A client-side Fabric mod for Minecraft **26.1.2-26.3**. QoL tweaks and visual upgrades.

**Requires Fabric API and Fabric language Kotlin**

Open the menu in game with `/tweaky`. Drag HUD elements around with `/tweaky hud`.

## Features

### General
- **Auto Sprint** - automatically sprints for you.
- **Auto Update** - keeps Tweaky up to date automatically.
- **Chat** - chat tweaks: Ctrl + click to copy messages, and timestamps.
- **Chat Waypoints** - click coordinates in chat to drop a temporary waypoint.
- **Fly Speed** - change how fast you fly in creative mode.
- **Party Chat** - your messages goes through the relay instead of hypixel.
- **Snappy Tappy** - keeps you moving when pressing opposing direction keys (no dead stop).
- **Time Changer** - change the client-side world time (day, noon, night, real time, and more).

### Visual
- **Animations** - reposition and rescale your hand view-model.
- **Block Overlay** - customizable block selection overlay (outline, fill, or both, with your own colors).
- **Camera** - full bright plus the 1.8-style sneak height (visual only).
- **Cosmetics** - custom player names & sizes, synced live from the cosmetics server.
- **Dark Mode** - darken the screen with an adjustable tint.
- **Dynamic Lights** - held torches and glowing items light up their surroundings.
- **Hurt Flash** - customize the red damage flash on entities.
- **Low Fire** - lower the first-person fire overlay so it stops blocking your view.
- **Map Player Heads** - show player heads instead of arrows on maps.
- **Nametag Tweaks** - tweak player nametags, including hiding the black background.
- **No Fog** - remove fog for a clear view at any distance.
- **Particle Control** - reduce or disable particles by category or type.
- **Render Optimizer** - hide useless entities and effects (falling blocks, lightning, XP orbs, and more).
- **Zoom** - hold a key to zoom in; scroll to adjust. Fully configurable.

### HUD
- **Clock Display** - show the system time on screen.
- **Coordinates HUD** - show your coordinates, facing direction & biome.
- **CPS Display** - show your left and right clicks per second.
- **FPS Display** - show the game's FPS.
- **Freeze Display** - show how long the server froze past a chosen threshold.
- **Inventory HUD** - draw your inventory, armor and potion effects on screen. Heavily customizable: per-part toggles, slot size/spacing/scale, backgrounds, slot colors, grid outlines, item counts and effect options.
- **Item Count HUD** - show how many of the held item you're carrying.
- **Scoreboard** - draw a custom, styled scoreboard in place of the vanilla one.
- **TPS Display** - show the server's ticks per second.

### GUI
- **Borderless Windowed** - borderless fullscreen window.
- **GUI Move** - move around while in containers; hold a key to walk with chat open.
- **Hide Recipe Book** - hide the recipe book button in inventory GUIs.
- **Inventory Search** - search your inventory, with math support.
- **No Cursor Reset** - keep the mouse position when opening a new GUI.
- **Remove Loading Screen** - skip loading screens on world joins & resource pack switches.
- **Screenshots Menu** - a screenshots gallery added to the pause & title menus.
- **Scrollable Tooltip** - scroll and scale long tooltips.
- **Slot Lock** - lock inventory slots against misclicks.

### Sound
- **Arrow Hit Sound** - play a custom sound when your arrow lands a hit.
- **Classic Hurt** - replace the player hurt sound with the classic "oof".
- **Mono Audio** - play all game audio through a single channel.
- **Satisfying Breaks** - play a satisfying sound on every block break.
- **Sound Manager** - adjust the volume of every individual sound in the game.

### Interface
- **Click GUI** - configure the settings menu itself (click sounds, and more).
- **Super Secret Settings** - cycle through fun post-processing shaders.
